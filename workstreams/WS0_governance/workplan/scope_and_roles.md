# Scope and roles

The engagement connects fuel scenarios to logistics, storage and commercially accessible opportunities. The reusable model remains client-agnostic. This brief records agreed workstreams, not commercial approval.

Manish is the analyst, available 100% across workstreams. Nigel is modelling lead; Henry is reviewer. Review timing and tolerances remain to be agreed; business approval is a separate release gate.

Scope includes SACU with Namibia/Walvis Bay focus, and screening of East, West, North Africa and SADC excluding SACU. Detailed country modelling follows evidence and priorities. The Vopak template and current kickoff deck are available in pptx/.

## Infrastructure decision

The user authorised [WS5 infrastructure and logistics](../../WS5_infrastructure_logistics/README.md) as a distinct workstream. It covers compatible routes, terminal throughput, usable storage, inventory requirements and access. Start with a South Africa inventory and one evidenced Durban-to-inland-Gauteng route. The engine is not implemented yet. Gas infrastructure informs production dependencies; gas-market modelling and detailed engineering are outside the first deliverable.

## Kickoff decisions

Review fuel and infrastructure baselines, select the first evidenced route, settle product and geographic mappings, agree data access, review checkpoints and tolerances, and prioritise scope against shared team capacity. The WS5 brief contains owners, input contracts, acceptance criteria and unresolved evidence.
