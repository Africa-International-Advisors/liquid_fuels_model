What the first implementation establishes

This pack contains staged historical evidence, source-linked supply findings and existing-engine diagnostics. It does not present a calibrated new forecast, actual Vopak market share or an investment recommendation.

On the matched departmental series, 2013-2023 petrol sales changed by -19.0% and diesel by +8.6%. The historical story therefore needs separate fuel trajectories.

Eskom reported OCGT diesel-and-kerosene use changed by -39.9% from FY2024 to FY2025. This is Eskom-only fuel use; it does not measure all private backup generation.

Both existing scenarios ran in the initial source audit. Only 29 of the original 72 declared blocks were requested by the engine; registered presentation snapshots are not automatically active forecast levers. A malformed vehicle-source metadata file was repaired without altering its evidence.

SAPREF / SANPC

Source finding: CEF describes phased redevelopment: initial terminal/depot/import functionality, then refining at approximately 400,000 bbl/day, with a longer-term 400,000-650,000 bbl/day objective subject to investment and approvals. Primary source

Interpretation: A terminal/import phase can precede refining; do not replace imports with full refinery output on announcement.
Remaining work: FID, dates, ramp-up, product slate and financing. This release does not establish a 2033 commissioning date.

Natref

Source finding: Sasol's 1 September 2026 update reports a mid-August downstream-unit outage coinciding with another unit's planned shutdown; continuity measures described through end September. Primary source

Interpretation: Evidence of operating risk, not permanent closure. FY2026 reported production improvement does not remove current outage risk.
Remaining work: Post-September operating status; outage duration, product losses, Prax/ownership and future investment.

Secunda / MRG

Source finding: Sasol confirms technical feasibility of supplying Secunda MRG externally July 2028-June 2030 as Mozambican gas declines; implementation depends on regulatory approval, customer compatibility and investment decisions. Primary source

Interpretation: Treat coal/gasifier recovery and feedstock diversion as separate effects. User's liquid-fuel displacement hypothesis is not quantified by this release.
Remaining work: MRG PJ/year, internal gas balance, LNG/other replacement and marginal petrol/diesel output losses; updated approvals.

Mossgas / PetroSA

Source finding: PetroSA's published tender scope states GTL non-operation since December 2020, terminal adaptation to finished-product imports and proposed liquids-refinery recommissioning without the offshore/HTFT sections. Primary source

Interpretation: Use 'non-operating since December 2020' rather than confirmed permanent closure. Recommissioning proposal is not committed output.
Remaining work: Publication date, subsequent project approvals, timeline, supply/yields and operating status confirmation.

Transnet

Source finding: Government communication sets at least 250 Mt on the Transnet network by 2030; FY2025 Transnet results report 160.1 Mt actual. Primary source

Interpretation: Network ambition is not all Transnet-operated incremental freight and not all displaced road freight.
Remaining work: Reconcile policy target vintages; corridor/commodity detail and net road-to-rail transfer.
